I stand in a vast, darkened plain of dry, textured grass that stretches toward a low, distant horizon. Above me, an immense, turbulent supercell cloud dominates the sky, its heavy, charcoal-gray masses swirling in dense, layered formations. Jagged bolts of brilliant white lightning pierce the dark belly of the clouds, illuminating the hazy, rain-streaked atmosphere with a cold, electric glow. The air feels thick with moisture and the weight of the approaching storm.  Massive clouds drift and churn continuously across the sky, their heavy shapes rolling and twisting in a slow, rhythmic dance. Beneath the dark cloud base, curtains of heavy rain fall steadily, creating a persistent, translucent veil over the landscape. The tall grasses in the field sway in a constant, sweeping motion driven by the rising wind, while lightning repeatedly flashes within the storm clouds, casting fleeting, intense light across the moving sky.
