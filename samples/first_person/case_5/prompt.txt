I stand on a cobblestone street corner where grand, multi-story sandstone buildings flank a narrow road. The architecture is classical, featuring ornate wrought-iron balconies, tall rectangular windows with white frames, and textured stone facades in warm beige and cream tones. To the left, a building curves around the corner, its ground floor housing large glass windows and dark wooden doors. Potted plants sit near a small outdoor seating area. The street is paved with grey, rectangular cobblestones that catch the bright, direct sunlight, casting long, sharp shadows across the ground. The sky above is a clear, brilliant blue.  A lone pedestrian walks steadily away from me down the sidewalk on the right, their figure receding into the distance. Further down the street, small shadows of moving clouds drift across the building facades. The light shifts subtly as the sun moves, causing the long shadows on the cobblestones to stretch and creep slowly along the ground.
